**Author:** Head of Sports Medicine & Performance Analytics

**Review Cycle:** Weekly Clinical Sign-Off

## 1. Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR) Operational Thresholds

The ACWR represents the ratio between the acute training load (rolling 7-day fatigue) and chronic training load (rolling 28-day fitness). All GPS and accelerometer loads must adhere to these clinical boundaries:

- **Zone 1: Under-training / Deconditioning (`ACWR < 0.80`)**
  - **Clinical Risk:** Decreased tissue tolerance and aerobic detraining; elevated vulnerability upon return to high-intensity match play.
  - **Action:** Prescribe progressive aerobic conditioning and supplementary eccentric hamstring/quadriceps loading.

- **Zone 2: Optimal "Sweet Spot" (`0.80 <= ACWR <= 1.30`)**
  - **Clinical Risk:** Lowest statistical relative risk of non-contact soft tissue injuries (< 1.5x baseline).
  - **Action:** Full training clearance. Player eligible for 90-minute competitive selection.

- **Zone 3: Elevated Risk / Cautionary (`1.30 < ACWR <= 1.50`)**
  - **Clinical Risk:** Approaching neuromuscular fatigue threshold; microtrauma accumulation in musculoskeletal junctions. Relative injury risk elevated 2.0x to 2.5x.
  - **Action:** Mandatory load curtailment by 15%–25%. Restricted from high-speed sprint overload drills (>25 km/h).

- **Zone 4: Critical Danger / Spike Zone (`ACWR > 1.50`)**
  - **Clinical Risk:** Exponential spike in soft-tissue failure probability (hamstring, groin, calf, patellar tendon). Relative risk exceeds 3.5x to 4.5x.
  - **Action:** Immediate training suspension or substitution with off-feet conditioning (pool, watt-bike). Maximum 50% technical drill participation. Minimum 48-hour recovery window.

## 2. Sleep Architecture & Recovery Deficit Criteria

Sleep is the primary physiological recovery window for protein synthesis, glycogen resynthesis, and cognitive reaction time restoration:

- **Baseline Requirement:** Elite athletes require 8.0 to 9.5 hours of restorative sleep per 24-hour cycle.
- **Moderate Sleep Debt (`6.0 <= Hours < 7.0`):**
  - Cumulative cortisol elevation, impaired glucose tolerance, and reduced tendon stiffness.
  - **Protocol:** Supplementary 45-minute afternoon nap required; hydration and magnesium glycinate supplementation.
- **Severe Sleep Deprivation (`Hours < 6.0`):**
  - Neuro-mechanical reaction delays; 1.7x higher incidence of acute non-contact ligament and tendon sprains.
  - **Protocol:** Mandated cognitive and visual testing prior to gym lifting. Restrict plyometrics and max-effort accelerations.

## 3. Subjective Wellness Composite Red Flags

Daily subjective wellness survey evaluated across four 10-point scales (Total composite normalized to 1–10, where 10 is optimal and 1 is critically compromised):

- **Composite Red Flag (`Composite Score < 5.0`):**
  - Requires immediate one-on-one medical consultation before entering the training pitch.
- **Specific Domain Triggers:**
  - **Muscle Soreness > 7.0/10 (High localized pain):**
    - Indicates unrecovered DOMS or emerging grade-1 myofascial strain.
    - Mandatory ultrasound / manual palpation by Lead Physiotherapist.
  - **Fatigue > 7.0/10:**
    - Autonomic nervous system suppression.
    - Reduce high-speed running volume by at least 30%.
  - **Stress & Mood < 4.0/10:**
    - Psychosomatic strain correlates with increased muscle tension and elevated injury frequency.

---

## 4. GPS & High-Speed Running (HSR) Volume Limits

- **HSR (> 21.0 km/h) Weekly Spike:**
  - An increase of greater than **20% week-on-week** in HSR distance triggers an automatic **Hamstring Strain Red Flag**.
  - Player restricted to technical passing circuits; no max-velocity sprints.
- **Sprint Distance (> 25.2 km/h):**
  - Player must not exceed 250 meters in a single training session following a match within 72 hours.

---

## 5. Prior Injury History Multipliers

- **Recent Injury Window (< 90 Days):**
  - Previous muscle strain (hamstring, adductor, calf) increases re-injury vulnerability by 300%.
  - Any player with a previous injury within 90 days and an `ACWR > 1.25` is automatically upgraded to **High Risk**.
- **Multi-Injury History (>= 3 injuries in career):**
  - Requires personalized warm-up and prophylactic eccentric resistance protocol prior to every session.

---

## 6. Tiered Medical Clearance Protocols

1. **Tier 1 — Full Clearance (`Cleared`):**
   - ACWR within 0.80–1.30. Sleep >= 7.0h. Wellness >= 6.0. No acute pain.
   - Cleared for match selection and unrestricted team training.

2. **Tier 2 — Modified Training (`Modified Training`):**
   - ACWR 1.30–1.50 OR Moderate Sleep Debt OR Wellness 5.0–6.0.
   - Restricted drill participation: No contact, no max-speed sprints, non-weight-bearing cardiovascular work.
   - Re-evaluation within 24 hours.

3. **Tier 3 — Mandatory Rest (`Rest Mandated`):**
   - ACWR > 1.50 OR Severe Sleep Deprivation (< 6.0h) + High Soreness (> 7.0) OR Prior injury recurrence symptoms.
   - Complete suspension from pitch training. Intensive physiotherapeutic recovery (contrast therapy, manual soft tissue release, hyperbaric oxygen).
   - Requires formal sign-off by Club Doctor before returning to Tier 2.

## 7. Mandatory Clinical Sign-Off Requirement

**CRITICAL RULE:** No AI model, predictive algorithm, or automated dashboard can independently clear or bench an athlete. The outputs generated by the predictive engine serve strictly as clinical decision-support telemetry. Final determination rests exclusively with the Club Doctor or Lead Physiotherapist.
